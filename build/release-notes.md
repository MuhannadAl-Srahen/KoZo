KoZo 1.12.0

Achievements tracked in more places, including shared Steam accounts, and KoZo now suggests what to play next.

New in 1.12.0
• Smart suggestions: set a game to Playing and KoZo offers to plan it for this month. Finish one and it offers the next game in your plan. At the start of a month it offers to move last month's unfinished games, and games left on Playing for three weeks can move to On hold, with Undo. Each one has Not now and Don't ask again, and Settings, General can turn them on or off. Never during a game.
• Shared Steam accounts: achievements someone else unlocked on a shared or rented account before you got there are listed on the game's page, with what each one asks. Tick the ones you've done with I did these too.
• Achievements between games: an unlock Steam writes after the game has closed (EA games pass them to Steam late, Offline Mode sends them once online) now counts within seconds, instead of at the next start.
• More achievement files found: games with no launcher are read from every emulator folder in Public Documents and ProgramData, from the save folder an emulator's own settings choose, and in any text format.
• Achievements you can't see: when a game with no launcher has been played a while and its emulator has saved nothing, its page says so and lets you mark the ones you earned.
• On hold tab: the Library has its own tab for games On hold, and Current shows the games you're playing and the ones you haven't started yet.
• Discounts: a game's window can take it off your Game List again, and prices are the ones Steam charges in your country.

Fixed in 1.12.0
• A finished game removed from the Library stays on the Finished page and in Your Year.
• KoZo no longer flashes its old purple look when it starts.
• Scan PC no longer lists a game's extra parts, like a copy of its program in a subfolder, as games of their own.
• A game taken off your Game List leaves Discounts' On your Game List at once.
• Discount prices no longer show in US dollars when Windows is set to the US but Steam sells in another country.
